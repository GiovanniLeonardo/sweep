# Sweep Privacy Notice (MVP draft)

Last updated: 2026-11-07

## What this app does
Sweep displays a camera preview for manual inspection, scans nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements when you initiate a scan, and stores inspection notes and checklist progress locally on your device.

## Data handled
- Camera: live preview only in the current MVP. Sweep does not record or upload video from the inspection screen.
- Bluetooth: nearby BLE advertisement metadata such as device identifier, advertised name, signal strength and service UUIDs, displayed locally during a scan.
- Local records: room name, checklist completion, review markers and inspection history are stored in app-local device storage.

## Sharing
Sweep does not include an account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK or cloud sync in this MVP. If you choose Share / Export, your device's operating-system share sheet sends the text you selected to the app or person you choose.

## Permissions
Camera permission is used for the live preview. Bluetooth permission is used for nearby BLE scanning. You can revoke permissions in your device settings.

## Retention and deletion
Local records remain on your device until you clear the inspection history, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Device review markers and checklist progress can be reset in-app.

## Important
This is a development-stage privacy notice, not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review. Update this notice and the app-store privacy disclosures before public release, including any future analytics, crash reporting, accounts, cloud services or additional data collection.
